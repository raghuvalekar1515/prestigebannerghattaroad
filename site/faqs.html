Prestige Bannerghatta Road FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about “Prestige Bannerghatta Road” project.

What is Prestige Bannerghatta Road?

An upcoming community of residential plots and independent villas by Prestige Group on about 104 acres off Bannerghatta Road in South Bengaluru. Register now to receive the plot sizes, villa plans and prices first, with priority access at launch.

Where is Prestige Bannerghatta Road located?

Off Bannerghatta Road in South Bengaluru, near Bilvaradhahalli. Bannerghatta Biological Park is about 7 km away, Royal Meenakshi Mall about 8 km, Apollo Hospitals and IIM Bangalore about 11 km and Electronic City Phase 1 about 18 km.

What can I buy at Prestige Bannerghatta Road?

Residential plots, on which to build a home of your own, and independent villas. Plot sizes, villa configurations and areas will be announced at launch.

What is the price of Prestige Bannerghatta Road?

Prices will be announced at launch. Register your interest to receive the price list first, with priority access at launch.

How large is the project?

About 104 acres, planned as a low-rise neighbourhood of plots and villas. The master plan will be shared at launch.

What amenities are planned?

The community is being planned around a clubhouse, a swimming pool and a children’s pool, a gym with yoga and meditation spaces, courts for tennis, badminton and basketball, cricket practice, walking, jogging and cycling paths, gardens, play areas, a senior citizens’ garden and a pet park. The final list will be shared at launch.

Who is the developer?

Prestige Group, founded in 1986 and one of India’s leading real estate developers, with more than 300 projects completed across over 200 million sq.ft. It is the only real estate developer in India graded CRISIL DA1+. Its South Bengaluru homes include Prestige Southern Star, part of a township of more than 30 acres in Akshayanagar.
