About the project

What is Prestige Bannerghatta Road?

An upcoming community of residential plots and independent villas by Prestige Group on about 104 acres off Bannerghatta Road in South Bengaluru. Register now to receive the plot sizes, villa plans and prices first, with priority access at launch.

How large is the project?

About 104 acres, planned as a low-rise neighbourhood of plots and villas. The master plan will be shared at launch.

Is Prestige Bannerghatta Road a plotted layout or a villa project?

It is both. The community combines residential plots, sold as land for you to build on, with independent villas that Prestige designs and builds. Both share one planned neighbourhood of about 104 acres near Bilvaradhahalli, with the same clubhouse, pools, courts and gardens, and there are no apartment towers in the plan. When will plots and villas be handed over?

The handover timelines for plots and for villas will be confirmed at launch. Ask for the date that applies to your exact plot or villa, and check that the agreement states it before you pay.

How does buying a plot at Prestige Bannerghatta Road work?

You buy the land and build the house yourself, choosing the architect, the design and the timing, inside a community planned with a clubhouse, gardens, courts and paths. Before you commit, ask whether the layout sets design rules for homes, such as height limits or a common look for the front, whether building must start by a set date, and when the plot is handed over so that construction can begin.

What does buying an independent villa include?

Here a villa means a complete home: Prestige draws up the design, builds the house on a plot of its own and hands both over to you together, for one price. The configurations, plot areas and built areas of each villa type come at launch, so ask for the plan and the cost sheet of the exact villa you like before you decide.

Should I choose a plot or a villa?

Choose a plot if you want to design the house yourself, or to build later or in stages as your budget allows, and you are ready to manage construction. Choose a villa if you would rather have a finished home, one price and one handover. Plot owners and villa owners at Prestige Bannerghatta Road share the same planned clubhouse, pools, courts and gardens, so the decision rests on your budget, your time and how involved you want to be.

What is the difference between plot area, carpet area and super built-up area?

Plot area measures land: everything inside the plot’s boundary. A villa adds a house, and houses are measured three ways: carpet area is the floor you can furnish, built-up area also counts the walls, and super built-up area loads on a portion of shared spaces too. When the Prestige Bannerghatta Road plans and price list come out, check which area each figure uses, and in which unit, so that you compare like with like.

What will the cost sheet show?

A cost sheet is the full price of one specific plot or villa. Expect it to give the plot number or villa type with its area and unit, the base rate and base price, any premium for position, any clubhouse, amenity or infrastructure charges, any maintenance deposit, and taxes. Stamp duty and registration are extra over the price. At Prestige Bannerghatta Road, request it for one named plot or villa rather than settling for a price band.

What payment plans will be offered?

The payment plans come out alongside the price list at launch, and a plot and a villa may not share one schedule. Villa buyers commonly pay in steps that follow the building work, from foundation to handover. Plot buyers may see payments linked to progress on the layout’s roads and services, ending with the sale deed. Before choosing, count the instalments and note which event releases each one.

Can I get a home loan for a plot or a villa?

Yes, but the loan type differs. For a villa, a standard home loan is the usual route, with the bank paying out as construction moves forward. For bare land, banks offer plot loans, and some offer a composite loan that funds the land now and the house later. Each lender sets its own terms. Get your eligibility checked before Prestige Bannerghatta Road launches so that a good plot or villa does not slip away while the paperwork catches up.

What is an expression of interest, and do I have to pay for one?

An expression of interest records that you want a plot or villa before allotment opens, and some developers take a refundable deposit with it. Registering your interest through this website is different: it is free and it is not a booking. Should Prestige Bannerghatta Road invite expressions of interest against a deposit, confirm the payee, the refund terms and timing, and how the amount is credited towards the plot or villa you finally pick.

What should I check before paying a token?

Know precisely which plot or villa the token is for, with its area and a cost sheet. Pay only into the developer’s account as named on the booking papers; this website never handles money. Find out whether you get the token back if you withdraw, how soon and minus what charges, and read the payment schedule and the handover date. At Prestige Bannerghatta Road most of these answers come at launch, so wait until you have them in writing.
