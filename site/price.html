How plot and villa prices differ at Prestige Bannerghatta Road

A plot is priced on its land. Expect the price list to give a rate for the plot area and the area of each plot, so that the price of a plot is its rate multiplied by its area, plus the charges on the cost sheet. Designing and building your home on it is a separate cost, paid at your own pace.

A villa is priced as a finished home on its own land: the price covers the plot under it and the house built on it. Ask whether the price list quotes each villa type as one figure or as a rate on an area, and which area that is, because the villa configurations and their areas are only announced at launch.

So a plot and a villa here are not compared on price alone. Set a plot’s price plus your own design and building cost, and the time it takes, against a villa’s price for similar land and built space.

What the Prestige Bannerghatta Road cost sheet will list

A cost sheet is the line-by-line price of one specific plot or villa. When the price list comes out at launch, ask for the cost sheet of the exact plot number or villa you are considering, not a price range. Expect it to show:

  • the plot number or the villa type, with its area and the unit the area is measured in
  • the base rate and the base price
  • any premium for a preferred position, such as a corner or a plot facing the central greens, if one is charged
  • any charges for the clubhouse, amenities or infrastructure
  • any maintenance deposit or advance maintenance
  • applicable taxes
  • stamp duty and registration, which are extra over the price

Any line marked as to be confirmed should be confirmed in writing before you pay anything.

Payment plans for Prestige Bannerghatta Road plots and villas

Payment plans will be shared with the price list at launch. Plots and villas tend to follow different schedules, so read the plan for the kind of home you want.

For a villa, a common structure is a construction-linked plan. After the booking amount, each instalment falls due when a stage of the villa is completed, such as the foundation, the structure, the finishes and handover, so you pay as your home is built rather than all at once.

For a plot there is no house to build in stages, so instalments are usually tied to the development of the layout’s roads and services and to the sale deed. Ask how many instalments there are, what each one is tied to, and when the plot will be handed over for you to start building.

Home loans for a plot or a villa at Prestige Bannerghatta Road

Because Prestige Bannerghatta Road sells both plots and villas, the loan you need depends on what you buy. A villa is a finished home, so it is financed with a home loan, with the money released in step with the construction-linked plan.

A plot is land without a home on it yet. It is financed with a plot loan, or with a composite loan that covers the plot and the house you build on it later; terms differ from lender to lender. If you plan to build soon after buying, a composite loan keeps the land and the construction under one loan.

Either way, check your eligibility before launch, so that you can act on the plot or villa you want once the price list is out. Your lender will ask for the cost sheet and the project documents, which you can request at launch.
