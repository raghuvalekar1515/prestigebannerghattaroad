A low-rise plan on about 104 acres

Prestige Bannerghatta Road is planned as a low-rise neighbourhood of plots and villas, not as apartment towers. That changes what its master plan looks like: instead of tower footprints around a podium, expect streets lined with plots and villas, with the clubhouse, courts and gardens set among them.

On land of about 104 acres, the plan also decides how far each home is from the clubhouse, the gardens and the entrance. Those distances, more than the total acreage, shape everyday life in a community of plots and villas.

A low-rise plan also decides what you see from home. With no towers, the outlook from a plot or villa is the street, the neighbouring homes and the nearest garden, so the arrangement of streets and greens on the plan matters as much as the size of the home.

How to read the Prestige Bannerghatta Road master plan

When the plan is released at launch, read it in this order:

  • Find the north arrow and the scale, so that you can tell which way each plot faces and how far apart things are.
  • Trace the entrance and the main roads, then the smaller streets that lead off them.
  • Read the legend, which tells the plots, villas, clubhouse, courts, greens and paths apart.
  • Find the clubhouse, the pools and the central greens, and see which streets are closest to them.
  • Find the plots or villas you are interested in, and note what is around them on every side.

A master plan is indicative until the final layout is confirmed. Read it as a guide to how the community is arranged, and confirm the details of your own plot or villa in writing.

Plots and villas in the Prestige Bannerghatta Road master plan

The master plan will show where the plots are and where the villas are: whether they sit in separate parts of the site or along the same streets, and how each relates to the gardens and the clubhouse.

For a plot buyer, the plan shows the plot’s shape and the road it faces. For a villa buyer, it shows which villa type stands where. In both cases the home next door, the view from the front and the walk to the nearest green appear only on the master plan, never on a price list.

If you are buying a plot to build on later, the plan also shows the neighbouring plots, whose owners may build before or after you do.

Green spaces in the Prestige Bannerghatta Road master plan

The planned green spaces are central greens, themed gardens and walking, jogging and cycling paths, along with play areas, a senior citizens’ garden and a pet park. On the plan, check whether the greens are gathered in the centre or spread through the streets, and how far your plot or villa is from the nearest one.

The artistic impressions show a shared landscape spine with a garden pavilion under old trees, and tree-lined residential streets. Look on the master plan for where such spaces fall, since impressions are for representation only.

Questions to ask about the Prestige Bannerghatta Road master plan

  • How much of the land is open space, and how much is roads?
  • Is the community being developed in phases, and which part comes first?
  • Where are the entrance and exit, and is there more than one?
  • Where are the clubhouse, pools and courts relative to the plots and villas?
  • How wide are the main roads and the internal streets?
  • When will the roads, services and amenities be ready?

These details will be confirmed at launch. Ask for them in writing with your cost sheet.
