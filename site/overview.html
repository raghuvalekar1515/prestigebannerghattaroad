Prestige Bannerghatta Road at a glance

Prestige Bannerghatta Road is Prestige Group’s upcoming community of residential plots and independent villas on about 104 acres near Bilvaradhahalli, off Bannerghatta Road in South Bengaluru. It is planned as a low-rise neighbourhood, with a clubhouse, pools, courts, gardens and walking and cycling paths among the plots and villas.

The project is at the upcoming stage. The plot sizes, villa configurations, prices, payment plans and master plan will be announced at launch, and every detail on this website is indicative, as of 21 October 2026.

What Prestige plans on about 104 acres

The land, about 104 acres, is planned as a whole neighbourhood rather than a single block. Prestige plans it as a low-rise community of residential plots and independent villas: streets and gardens at ground level instead of towers, and homes that each stand on their own land.

The master plan will show how the land is shared between plots, villas, roads, the clubhouse and the green spaces. The planned green spaces include central greens, themed gardens and walking, jogging and cycling paths. The master plan page explains how to read the plan when it is released.

Plots or villas: choosing your home at Prestige Bannerghatta Road

The project offers two ways to own. A residential plot is land on which you design and build a home of your own, on your own timeline. An independent villa is a home that Prestige designs and builds on its own land, finished when it is handed over.

Plot sizes, villa configurations and areas will be announced at launch. Until then the choice is about how you want to own: control over design and timing with a plot, or a finished home with a villa. The plots and villas page sets the two side by side.

Where Prestige Bannerghatta Road is

The project is near Bilvaradhahalli, off Bannerghatta Road, Bengaluru 560083. Along Bannerghatta Road, IIM Bangalore and Apollo Hospitals are both about 11 km away by road. Royal Meenakshi Mall at Hulimavu is about 8 km away, Konanakunte Cross on Kanakapura Road about 10 km and Electronic City Phase 1 about 18 km.

Bannerghatta Biological Park, about 7 km away, is the nearest of the landmarks listed on this website. The location page has every distance, a map link and what to check on a site visit.

Planned amenities at Prestige Bannerghatta Road

The amenities are planned for every age group. Indoors, there is a clubhouse with a residents’ lounge, a multipurpose hall and indoor games, and a gym with yoga and meditation spaces. For sport and swimming there are courts for tennis, badminton and basketball, cricket practice, a swimming pool and a children’s pool. Outdoors, central greens, themed gardens and paths for walking, jogging and cycling run alongside play and toddler areas, a senior citizens’ garden and a pet park.

The list is planned, not final; Prestige will share the final list at launch. The amenities page groups them and lists what to ask about each.
