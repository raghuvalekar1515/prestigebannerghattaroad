Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani: location

Prestige Bannerghatta Road is near Bilvaradhahalli, off Bannerghatta Road. DNR Jigani is at Jigani, on the Bannerghatta-Anekal road in Anekal taluk, south of Electronic City; DNR Jigani is a working name, and its exact site is shared at launch.

The distances below are approximate road distances, from Bilvaradhahalli for Prestige Bannerghatta Road and from Jigani bus stand for DNR Jigani.

DNR Jigani is closer to Electronic City, Narayana Health City and Treamis World School, and the Jigani and Bommasandra industrial estates are within about 3.1 km of Jigani bus stand.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani: land and scale

Prestige Bannerghatta Road is much the larger site: about 104 acres, against about 21 acres at DNR Jigani. DNR Jigani is planned with about 200 plots; the number of plots and villas at Prestige Bannerghatta Road will be confirmed at launch, with its master plan.

Size changes the feel of a community. A larger site has room for more shared spaces and more households to share them; a smaller one has fewer of both. Compare the layouts at launch to see how each divides its land between homes, roads and shared spaces.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani: what you can buy

DNR Jigani is a plotted development: you buy a plot and build your own home. Prestige Bannerghatta Road offers residential plots too, and adds independent villas that Prestige designs and builds.

Neither has announced its plot sizes, and Prestige Bannerghatta Road has not yet announced its villa configurations. If you want a finished home rather than a plot, Prestige Bannerghatta Road is the one of the pair that offers it.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani: prices and payment plans

Prices for both projects are announced at launch. Prestige Bannerghatta Road will share its payment plans with its price list, and DNR Jigani’s price list and payment plan are also released at launch. Once both are out, compare them on the cost sheets of plots of similar size, line by line.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani: amenities

Prestige Bannerghatta Road has a planned list: a clubhouse with a residents’ lounge, a multipurpose hall and indoor games, a swimming pool and a children’s pool, a gym with yoga and meditation spaces, courts for tennis, badminton and basketball, cricket practice, walking, jogging and cycling paths, central greens and themed gardens, play areas, a senior citizens’ garden and a pet park. Its final list comes at launch.

DNR Jigani’s amenities are announced at launch, so there is no list to compare yet. Set the final lists, and the clubhouse sizes, side by side when both projects launch.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road or DNR Jigani: who each one suits

Prestige Bannerghatta Road suits buyers who want to live off Bannerghatta Road, with Bannerghatta Biological Park about 7 km away and IIM Bangalore and Apollo Hospitals about 11 km; who want the choice of a plot or a finished villa; and who prefer a large community of about 104 acres with a planned clubhouse, pools, courts and gardens.

DNR Jigani suits buyers who work in the Jigani or Bommasandra industrial estates or at Electronic City, about 11 km from Jigani bus stand; who want Narayana Health City and the Yellow Line metro at Bommasandra about 7.5 km away; and who prefer a smaller layout of about 200 plots on about 21 acres.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road and DNR Jigani: questions for both launches

  • The list of plot sizes, with dimensions, and the number of plots in each size.
  • The rate, and every charge outside it on the cost sheet.
  • The share of the land left for roads, parks and common areas.
  • The clubhouse: its size, what it holds and when it opens.
  • The handover date for plots, and when maintenance charges start.
  • For Prestige Bannerghatta Road, the villa configurations and their built areas as well.

Ask both projects the same questions and set the answers side by side before you pay a token to either.

Prestige Bannerghatta Road vs DNR Jigani summary

Both are upcoming South Bengaluru communities where plots will be sold, with sizes and prices announced at launch.
