How Prestige Group began

Prestige began in Bengaluru in 1986 with Prestige Court, its first commercial project. Its first residential development was Prestige Oakwood.

The business started as a partnership firm and was later converted into a company, incorporated in Karnataka in 1997. That company, Prestige Estates Projects Limited, is the one behind the Prestige brand today.

Its shares were listed in October 2010, on the National Stock Exchange of India under the symbol PRESTIGE and on BSE under scrip code 533274.

What Prestige Group builds

Homes are one of several businesses. Prestige builds residential projects, commercial offices, retail malls and hotels, and it runs a property management and services business. In retail, Forum Koramangala marked its entry into organised retail.

In housing, its formats include high-rise apartments, integrated townships, a golf course community and plotted enclaves. Plotted enclaves are the format closest to the residential plots at Prestige Bannerghatta Road, which also offers independent villas.

Where Prestige Group builds homes

Prestige has a presence in 13 cities. Its residential projects, ongoing or upcoming, are in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, NCR, Goa, Kochi, Kozhikode, Mangaluru, Mysore, Ooty and Sakleshpur.

Bengaluru is where the company was founded and where its registered office stands, on Brunton Road. Prestige Bannerghatta Road adds a community of plots and villas on about 104 acres in the south of the city.

Plots and villas: the formats behind Prestige Bannerghatta Road

Prestige Bannerghatta Road brings residential plots and independent villas together on about 104 acres off Bannerghatta Road, near Bilvaradhahalli. Both have precedents in Prestige’s work: villas at Prestige Golfshire and Prestige White Meadows, apartments and villas together at Prestige Lakeside Habitat, and plotted enclaves among its residential formats.

For a buyer, that suggests questions worth asking at launch: how the villa plans here compare with the villas Prestige has built before, and how the plotted part of the community will be developed and handed over.
